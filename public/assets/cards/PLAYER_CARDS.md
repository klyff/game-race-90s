# RunnersCity Player Cards

Hand this file to the image agent. Each card is a 16-bit portrait in the same
language as `public/assets/cards/klyff.png`.

**Shared art rules (every card):**
- Always facing camera, chest-up, doing a face (big laugh, tongue out, snarl, smirk).
- Rock n' Roll Racing / GTA Vice City energy, 90s pixel figures, mixed with a few modern faces.
- Parallax LA / Santa Monica / Hollywood background (palm neon, pier, boulevard, garage, sunset).
- White 4px outer border. Front-facing. No three-quarter cheat.
- Always end the prompt with: **Make it in 16-bit like the card that is in public/assets/cards/klyff.png**

**Roster rules:**
- 21 regulars are locked when a career starts (KLYFF first). They race every planet.
- On planet 10 (Verdant Fault) the 5 JOKERS take the front of the field.
- Klyff is the game creator and the first face on CHOOSE YOUR FACE. He is a regular rival.

## KLYFF — Creator (first regular)

**Race name:** KLYFF
**Card file:** `klyff.png` / `klyff.txt`
**Role:** Game creator. The only man in RunnersCity with an original DeLorean.

BIO
Klyff walks the straight line and defends his business with a firm fist. On the wheel he is unbeatable. He loves staying with his family and eating barbecue, and he holds court at the pub with his car-prep crew. He is the only driver who owns an original DeLorean — and he doubled the motor. Bald, stubble, tank top, Cuban-link chain, tribal ink on one arm, flaming skull on the other. He built this circus and he still shows up to race it.

PHYSICAL / WARDROBE / SET
Muscular middle-aged man, bald, tanned, brown eyes, confident knowing smile. Black ribbed tank top, thick silver Cuban-link chain, tattoos on both arms (tribal right, yellow skull in red-orange flames left). Outlaw garage pub behind him: purple muscle car, neon OUTLAW Garage, parked bikes, LIVE FAST RIDE HARD. Always facing camera.
